## Concept and Decision

**Current concept and intended experience:** Inspired by how Paralympic athletes overcome challenges from handicaps, players too experience the chaos of being forced into minigames where they too have a handicap.

**Design question or decision:** Which game concept should I move forward with for the physical prototype?

**Selected concept and alternatives kept:** I'm not sure about "alternatives kept" but I selected Paralympic Minigames because a physical prototype would be simpler to create and the concept of torturing people was originally what I intended but this seemed like a more fun way to do it.

**Peer feedback that affected the decision, if received (otherwise say unavailable):** unavailable (I was planning on doing this concept from when I thought of it)
