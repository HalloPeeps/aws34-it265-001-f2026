# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

Copy this card for each of your two or three shortlisted concepts. Aim to explain each in about one minute. Use your [idea bank](./Idea-Bank.html) as a starting point.

## Pitch Card 1

**Working title:** Everything Hates You

**Player role and situation:** Players are to control characters from a top-down 2d third person shooter.

**Repeated decision or action:** Players can move around, shoot, or hide. Be the first to kill the opponent 3 times.

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** Best of 5 in turns of 1v1s.

**Hook:** The game is a 1v1, but could turn into a 100v1 or a 1v100 if someone keeps missing.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** Probably a DND panel with a protractor ruler (kind of like how you can use angles in virtual pool)

**Question I want listeners to answer:** "what's the best way you can turn the environment to my advantage?"


## Pitch Card 2

**Working title:** Paralymic Minigames

**Player role and situation:** Players are both the active oppressor and victim of the opponent.

**Repeated decision or action:** Hinder the opponent or pick a tool, then complete a minigame. 

**Goal, pressure, or ending:** First to fail a minigame while having no lives remaining is deemed the loser.

**Hook:** Minigames and Handicaps are randomized, but can still strategically used.

**Smallest useful physical prototype:** Hand drawn cards that use the cheapest items can be used to make a small prototype.
